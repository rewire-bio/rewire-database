## DreaMS extraction

`dreams-batch-spec.json` follows `BatchSpec`. It refers to existing source `evidence-official-116050b81d03a38e3b6f`, SHA-256 `4bbe2ecff0ad75944b3aec5131369c1b2f75ff6f4b5dc257487a274fdd7b0fef`. The proposed benchmark ID `discovery-benchmark-mist-canopus` does not yet exist and requires a sourced top-level record.

- Complete original comparison: FFN fingerprint, FFN contrastive, MIST fingerprint, MIST contrastive, MIST contrastive + fingerprint, DreaMS fingerprint (ours).
- Seven retrieval accuracy@k columns (k=1,5,10,20,50,100,200), printed as percentages; fingerprint cosine similarity is dimensionless. All directions higher-is-better.
- DreaMS is supervised fine-tuned fingerprint prediction, not frozen representation performance. Its top-1 printed result is 32.731 percent.
- Candidate positives share the true molecular InChIKey's first 14 characters; negatives share molecular formula. Candidates are ranked by fingerprint cosine similarity.
- All uncertainty, seeds and exact scored/eligible test counts are unreported in this table. The methods' approximate 8,000 spectra / 7,000 molecules describes the benchmark overall and must not be substituted for a test denominator.
- Three cosine-similarity dashes remain null/unreported and never become zero-valued results.
- Comparator results are reported by the DreaMS paper, but their original execution provenance is unresolved. Do not call them independent reproductions or new independent evidence until reviewed.
- `dreams-table-validation.json` records the second XML reconciliation. `dreams-complete-table.csv` preserves every printed cell and locator.

## Publication safeguards

Aliases establish family identity only. `variant_of` preserves retraining/species differences. `uses_model` keeps pipeline results separately navigable. Existing result IDs, scores and source origins must remain unchanged when adding associations. No reviewed association is proposed for VCC entries from names alone. All external-source files and intermediate extracts remain local; publication should copy only reviewed records and receipts into the release pipeline.

## Follow-up primary-source review

METAGENE-1: fetched the original arXiv v1 HTML and verified all 334 model-card score cells against Tables 2, 3 and 4. Section 5.3 establishes accuracy for eight classification tasks and V-measure for eight clustering tasks. `metagene-mteb-batch-spec.json` contains the complete 80 component scores (five methods on all 16 tasks). The 25 aggregate cells remain in `metagene-mteb-complete-review.json`; they do not become additional experiment counts. The source paper clearly states a label-fitted logistic-regression probe, so “zero-shot embeddings” must not be described as label-free evaluation. Original dataset and evaluator revision remain unreported.

The Table 2 pathogen detection metric is MCC, not accuracy. Table 4 uses MCC except COVID F1; its non-METAGENE comparator scores are explicitly adapted from Zhou et al. (2023). `metagene-methods-review.json` records these definitions, adaptation differences, label construction, denominators and unresolved displayed-scale/original-result-identity issues. Do not publish those remaining cells without those checks.

VCC: inspected the original saved leaderboard JSON, verified its stored SHA-256 and reviewed the previously unextracted `description` field. There are 41 relevant name/team matches. Four descriptions explicitly identify Arc Institute STATE or cite the State paper, supporting separately proposed author-declared pipeline `uses_model` links in `vcc-proposed-uses-edges.json`. These do not establish checkpoint equivalence or independently verify execution. `vcc-description-review.json` preserves descriptions and decisions without copying team-member information. The earlier blanket ambiguity statement is therefore narrowed: four have sourced pipeline identities; the rest retain identity gaps. No GEARS/scFoundation mapping with an explicit original-paper/implementation identifier was established.

The STATE checkpoint submission L11HGfhfp2YsWaMSMxKo explicitly reports zero of 300 targets covered and control fallback for all 900 groups. Its score must not be treated as actual STATE predictive performance; no family/evaluation association is proposed for it.
