# Batch 4 literature review

Reviewed 2026-09-15 UTC. This batch adds **24 distinct primary papers and 24 printed numerical result rows**: four papers in each of `dna-genomes`, `rna-transcriptomes`, `proteins-complexes`, `cells-tissues`, `microbes-communities`, and `molecular-interactions`. Each paper has one row, an exact table/row/column locator, the paper's DOI where available, a PMC primary-source URL, retrieval and review timestamps, publication status, and a protocol description. The full text was read from the official Europe PMC `fullTextXML` endpoint for each PMCID; no values were estimated from plots or synthesized from prose.

The launch dataset currently contains 76 papers because batches 2 and 3 were integrated into `papers.json`. All 24 batch-4 IDs, PMCIDs, and DOIs were checked against those 76 records before inclusion; the integrated launch set plus this batch contains 100 distinct paper IDs and primary-source URLs. The archived batch-2 and batch-3 files repeat records now in the launch dataset and should not be counted a second time.

Four papers are preprints (`arsenal-regulatory-dna-2026`, `plantcad2-2025`, `vaxign-esm-2024`, `gremln-2026`); all other records identify peer-reviewed venues. `evaluation_origin` is `author_reported` for 23 paper-owned results and `independent_paper` for the scGen row assessed in the AUPRC perturbation-method paper. No copied comparator value was included. The paper notes and CSV protocols distinguish foundation-model outputs from downstream systems using foundation-model features.

Interpretation requires care. The Hi-Enhancer, position-aware CNN, CUPID, DETIRE, PC-mer, and MDL4Microbiome rows are task-specific baselines, not biological foundation-model scores. The mRNA/protein-diversity result uses ProteinBERT to encode the protein partner; it should not be advertised as an RNA language-model score. CLAPE-SMB, ProtT5 binding-site, Vaxign/ESM, scGPT geometry, and ESM2_AMPS results belong to combined downstream systems. The ADAR-GPT row is from one liver validation set; the R3Design row is sequence recovery, a different task from structure prediction. The Cell-DINO F1 and several microbial scores are printed on 0–100 scales, while most other F1/AUROC values are fractions. Cross-paper ranking is inappropriate without aligned datasets, splits, units, and protocols.

Audit: the CSV was parsed after writing; all 24 result values were found in the named primary XML tables, each result points to its included paper and domain, and the combined main-plus-batch-4 IDs/DOIs/source URLs were checked for duplicates. The repository's main-data validator passed on the already integrated 76-paper dataset; batch 4 remains separate for review and integration.
