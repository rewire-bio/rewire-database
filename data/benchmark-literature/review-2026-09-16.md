Three interpretation details were corrected in both the integrated CSV and its retained batch-4 source: Pearson correlation is dimensionless, not a fraction; the ProtT5 row names the downstream ensemble classifier; and Cell-DINO's protocol explicitly records dataset-specific pretraining and the paper's limitation on generalization beyond the reported benchmarks.

Validation now rejects empty numerical cells rather than treating them as zero, malformed quoted CSV fields, impossible calendar dates, duplicate primary-source papers and result records, and mismatched paper/result provenance. The batch merger rejects truncated rows as well as extra cells. Regression tests exercise both the site loader and build validator.
